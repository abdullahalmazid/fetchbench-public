# Privacy

Fetchbench runs entirely on your computer. It has no account, no analytics and no ads,
and it does not send your data to the author or to any server of ours.

## What the app stores (on your PC only)

- Your download list, settings and logs in `%APPDATA%\com.fetchbench.desktop\`.
- Login details you type for a site that needs one, so the download can resume.
  They are stored in that same folder, unencrypted — don't use this on shared PCs.

## What the app sends over the internet

- Requests to the websites you download from (like any browser).
- If you install the optional tools, downloads from their official GitHub release pages.

## What the browser extension does

- When a download starts, it sends the link, file name, size, referring page, your browser's
  user agent and that site's cookies **to the Fetchbench app on your own computer**
  (`127.0.0.1`), so the download works with your login. Nothing leaves your PC.
- It remembers video stream addresses that pages load, only to offer the "Download video"
  button. This list is kept in the browser's temporary storage and cleared when the tab closes.
- It stores your two on/off switches in the browser.
